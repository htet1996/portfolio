Case study · SagaNote

AI Voice → Notion

Problem
Myanmar teams lose meeting decisions because manual note-taking is slow and English AI tools mis-transcribe local speech.
Solution
A voice-first pipeline using Gemini 2.5 Flash for transcription and Burmese translation, then auto-formatting and saving minutes to Notion.

  • AI transcription & Burmese translation
